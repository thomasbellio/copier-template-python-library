# NNNN. <Decision title>

ADRs are numbered in sequence and never rewritten. When a decision changes, write a new ADR
that supersedes the old one, and mark the old one's status "Superseded by NNNN".

## Status

Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN

## Context

What forces are at play: the problem, the constraints, and the options considered.

## Decision

What was decided, stated in full sentences ("We will ...").

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder as a result, including the costs accepted.
